WAV, 8-32 bit PCM or float, mono or stereo (mixed to mono), up to 60 s. Use the UW machines to play a slot (set its slot number on the Push or with the parameter sliders).

Setup

Audio: MachineModule sends its sound to Live over Link Audio. In Live, turn on Link and Link Audio, then give an audio track the input PushHack and pick the MachineModule channel. No other audio setting is needed.


  

    
    
  

  
External MIDI: notes 36-51 play tracks 1-16. CC 16-111 set track parameters (24 per track, four tracks per channel: tracks 1-4 on the base channel, 5-8 on the next, ...). CC 112 master volume, 113 tempo (30-157), 114 mute all. Program change selects a pattern. Clock, start and stop follow when Sync is MIDI clock.
